# Code of conduct

*[Deutsche Fassung](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.de.md)*

We want everyone involved in Knowstead to feel welcome and respected – regardless of experience, origin,
gender, identity, age, disability, appearance, religion or belief.

## What we expect

- a friendly, patient and factual tone – also when opinions differ,
- constructive feedback that addresses the matter, not the person,
- consideration for the fact that many contribute in their spare time,
- taking responsibility for your own mistakes and being willing to learn from them.

## What we do not tolerate

- insults, derogatory comments and personal attacks,
- harassment – in public or in private,
- publishing other people's private information without their consent,
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported confidentially to the people responsible for the repository: by e-mail to
<info@steintecdev.com>. All reports are reviewed confidentially and fairly. Possible consequences range
from a reminder to the removal of contributions and exclusion from the project.

This code of conduct is based on the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
