# Read confirmations

Policies, work instructions, safety briefings and privacy notices must not only be published but **demonstrably read**. With read confirmations you define who has to read a page and by when – and Knowstead collects the "read and understood" confirmations as evidence.

<!-- toc -->

## Requesting a read confirmation

Everyone with the **Manage space** permission (editorial team) can request one – in the page's **"…"** menu under **Read confirmation …**:

1. Choose the **target audience**: groups, individual people or **Everyone with read access** in this space. People without read access to the page are listed separately in the evidence.
2. Adjust the **confirmation text**, e.g. "I have read and understood the work instruction."
3. Set the **deadline in days**.
4. Optionally **Repeat** (e.g. every 12 months for annual briefings) and **Confirm again for new versions** – minor changes do not trigger a new round.
5. **Request read confirmation**.

The target audience is notified immediately (by email as well if delivery is set up).

> [!NOTE]
> Read confirmations are only available for published pages. Whoever joins the target audience later – for example by being added to a group – must confirm as well.

## Confirming

Whoever has to confirm sees a banner **"Please read and confirm by …"** on the page with the button **Read and understood**. After the click the page shows "Read and confirmed on … (version …)". Time, version and the confirmed text are stored.

All open mandatory documents are listed under **Read confirmations → For me** in the main menu – with a counter as soon as something is pending.

## Reminders

Knowstead reminds automatically, without anyone having to chase:

| When | Reminder |
| --- | --- |
| on request | "Please read and confirm" |
| three days before the deadline | "Read confirmation by …" |
| after the deadline | once: "Read confirmation overdue" |
| at the push of a button | **Remind open ones now** in the page dialog – at most once a day per person |

## Evidence and progress

In the **Read confirmation …** dialog and under **Read confirmations → Management** you see for each page:

- the **progress** ("12 of 15 confirmed"), overdue people and people without read access,
- the **evidence** with person, status (*Confirmed*, *Open*, *Overdue*, *No longer in target audience*), time, version and confirmed text,
- **Evidence as CSV** for auditors, quality management or HR.

**Start new round** resets all confirmations – everyone must confirm again and the deadline starts anew. **Remove requirement** ends the reminders; confirmations already given remain as evidence.

> [!TIP]
> For annual mandatory briefings choose "Repeat: every 12 months". Knowstead then starts the next round on its own and reminds again.
