# Content analytics

A knowledge base only stays useful if someone maintains it. **Content analytics** shows the editorial team what is being read, what is missing and what needs fixing – as a **work list**.

<!-- toc -->

## Who sees it

Content analytics is available in the main menu under **Content analytics**:

- **Space administrators** see the analysis of their own spaces.
- People with the global permission **View content analytics** see all spaces they may read – and are the only ones who see **searches without results**.

At the top you choose the **space** (or all spaces you may analyse) and the **period** (7 to 365 days).

## The five lists

| List | Shows | What you do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Most read** | views and number of readers per page | keep important pages especially up to date, link them prominently |
| **Unread** | pages nobody except their authors opened during the period | merge, link better or delete |
| **Searches without results** | search terms that never found anything | create missing pages (**Create page** uses the term as title) or add tags |
| **Orphaned** | pages no other page links to | link them from a suitable overview page |
| **Broken links** | links to deleted, unpublished or non-existent pages and attachments | fix the link or restore the target |

**Done** removes an entry from the work list. If the problem appears again later (e.g. a new broken link), it shows up again. Every list can be downloaded **as CSV**.

## Privacy

- Views are counted per page and day – **not** who read which page when.
- For search terms it is **not** stored who searched. Searches with results are not recorded.
- Views are deleted automatically after 400 days, search terms after 180 days.
