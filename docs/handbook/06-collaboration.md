# Collaboration

<!-- toc -->

## Comments

Below every page, people with at least the **Comment** role can discuss:

- Comments support formatting and **@mentions**. The list after the **@** first shows who is involved
  with the page (owner, creator, last editor, commenters) and marks people without access to the page –
  they would not learn about the mention.
- **Replies** form threads; a thread can be marked as **resolved** (and reopened).
- **Comments on text passages**: select a text passage in the view and click "Comment". The passage is quoted and highlighted in yellow in the document as long as the thread is open.

You can edit and delete your own comments; space administrators can moderate all comments.

## Watching and notifications

- **Watch page** (eye icon): you are informed about changes, comments, moves and deletions.
- **Watch space**: you additionally learn about new pages in the space.
- **Watch automatically**: by default you watch pages you create, edit or comment on (can be turned off in the profile).
- **Mentions** and **replies** to your comments always reach you.

Notifications appear immediately via the bell at the top right (live update) and – if the administrators have set up email delivery – additionally by email.

### Configuring email notifications

Under **Profile & settings → Preferences** you define how Knowstead reaches you by email:

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Immediately** | every notification also as an email |
| **Daily digest** | one email in the morning with everything you have not read yet |
| **No emails** | only the bell in Knowstead |
| **Only personal notifications by email** | only mentions, replies, reviews, assigned tasks and reminders – no emails about watched pages |
| **Reminder for due tasks** | how long before the due date you are reminded (on the day itself up to one week before) |

Every email contains a button that leads directly to the place in Knowstead. Emails are sent in the language you chose in your profile.

## Favourites and recently visited

Mark favourites with the star (or <kbd>S</kbd>). The home page shows your favourites, recently visited pages, open drafts and the latest activities.

## Working at the same time

At the top right Knowstead shows **who is currently reading the same page**. If a page is updated by someone else while you are reading, a notice with "Reload" appears.

**Editing together**: you can edit document pages at the same time – you see the others' cursors with names, every change appears immediately for everyone, and the shared state is saved continuously. Whoever is done **publishes** – this creates a new version. Details in the chapter **Editor and macros → Editing together**.

**Suggesting instead of editing**: on pages in suggestion mode, changes become suggestions that the author accepts or rejects – see **Editor and macros → Suggesting changes**.

For Markdown and code pages, each person edits their own draft; when publishing, Knowstead reliably detects editing conflicts based on the version number.
