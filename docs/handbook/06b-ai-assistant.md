# AI assistant

<!-- toc -->

If the administrators have connected an AI, Knowstead helps with writing and reading. The assistant only works at the push of a button – nothing is transferred without your click, and no result ends up in a page unchecked.

> [!WARNING]
> AI answers can contain mistakes. Check results before adopting them – especially numbers, names and deadlines.

## While writing

In the editor you find the **AI** button in the toolbar:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Improve language** | spelling, grammar and clarity – content stays the same |
| **Shorten** | to about half, without losing statements |
| **Translate** | into the chosen language; technical terms are preserved |
| **Summarise** | key points as bullets |
| **Create outline** | suggested headings with short explanations |
| **Derive tasks** | to-dos in the format "task – owner – deadline" |
| **Suggest title** | five title ideas |

If text is selected, the AI only works with the selection, otherwise with the whole page. The result appears in a **preview** first. From there you can:

- **Replace** (the selection is swapped),
- **Insert below**,
- **Copy** or
- **refine** it with an instruction, e.g. "more formal" or "as a table".

Undo works as always with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>.

## While reading

Via **… → AI assistant** on a page:

- **Summarise** – what is this page about?
- **Ask a question** – the AI answers only based on the page content and names the sections it relies on.
- **Tags** – suggestions you adopt as tags with one click.
- **Suggest title** and **Derive tasks**.

Results can be copied or put into the **clipboard** and inserted into another page later.

## Limits and privacy

- Only people in groups with the **Use AI features** permission see the assistant.
- The administrators define **which actions** are enabled and how many requests per person and day are possible. The remaining number is shown in the assistant.
- Only what you select or the open page is transferred – and only if you may read it anyway.
- Whether a cloud provider or a local model on your own network is used is decided by the administrators. A local model is recommended for confidential content.
